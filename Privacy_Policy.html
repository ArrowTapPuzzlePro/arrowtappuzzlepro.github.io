Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AKD Studio / D - AS Software ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you play ArrowTapPuzzle Pro (application package: com.akdstudio.arrowtappuzzlepro).

1. Information Collection & Usage

ArrowTapPuzzle Pro is designed to provide an offline, relaxing gaming experience. We do not require users to create an account, log in with an email address, or provide any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) to play the game.

2. Offline Game Progress & Storage

All gameplay progress, completed levels, unlocked themes (Classic, Night, Neon), earned coins, and user settings are stored locally on your device via standard local storage. If you clear the app data or uninstall the application, your locally stored progression may be reset.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep ArrowTapPuzzle Pro free for all players, the application may utilize certified third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Google Play Services). These third-party services may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data such as:

  • Anonymous Device identifiers (e.g. Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general coarse location (country/city level)
  • App performance and crash logs to improve stability

You can review Google's privacy practices at Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Device Permissions

ArrowTapPuzzle Pro may request standard Android permissions:

  • Vibration (Haptics): Used exclusively to deliver tactile physical feedback when arrows are tapped and cleared.
  • Network State & Internet Access: Used for Google Play license verification and serving non-intrusive advertisements or reward ads.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

ArrowTapPuzzle Pro is rated for all ages (3+). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our app features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
